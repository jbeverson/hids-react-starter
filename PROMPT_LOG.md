# Use case card and reflection

This file is what you hand in. Fill in both parts.

## Use case card
1. Who is the patient who would use this app?
2. What is the one task they are trying to do?
3. What data from the FHIR server would the app need?
4. What is one way the app could mislead or harm that person?

## Reflection
Write about one paragraph for each prompt. Prompts 2 and 3 need specific examples, so keep your Copilot chat history or take screenshots as you work.

1. **Why this app.** Who is the patient in your use case card, and why would this app be a good idea for them? Name one kind of patient it would not help or might frustrate, and say why.

2. **What the AI did.** Describe one prompt where Copilot gave you what you wanted and one where it did not. For each, say what you asked, what happened, and what you did next. If your app did not work, explain where it broke and what you think went wrong.

3. **One thing you checked yourself.** Pick one thing the app or the AI claimed, such as what the data contains, what the code does, or what a field means. Explain how you checked it against the real data or the code, and what you found.

4. **Before a real patient uses it.** What would have to be true before you would let a real patient use this app? Cover at least one safety or accuracy risk from your use case card, and who besides you should review the app. Then say what you would do differently the next time you build something with AI.
